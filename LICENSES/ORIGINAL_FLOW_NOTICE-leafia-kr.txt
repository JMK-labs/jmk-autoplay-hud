Revision 2 also ports the optional Phone Projection menu correction to the
authenticated Korean BtSettings APK/ODEX. Only Korean REGION=0 bypasses the

The builder adds one hash-gated Korean optional-menu row/delta. All original
optional rows and other regional data remain unchanged. KR data/selectors and
visible version/BUILD metadata are updated; license/provenance files are added.
Revision 3 integrates the existing DiPO wireless CP bridge with the authenticated
OEM PhoneProjection action/state interface. It registers one process-stable
action listener, binds its current service owner, scopes requests to that peer,
and publishes completion only after native finalization and live inactivity.
The BtSettings deletion hook retains an active wireless CP record until the
matching disconnected-status fanout finishes, then rechecks peer/profile/live
inactivity before invoking the original delete API. It preserves the existing
Korean menu selector. In revision 3 both apps used their existing original
installer parts and the other ten functional outputs remained identical to
revision 2 (revision 4 changes are listed below).
The existing owner retains pending-target and preference policy. No new owner
APK is installed and no AA active-projection guard is removed. A separately
reviewed lifecycle extension uses new metadata hooks and an execute-type USB
entry for verified updates and removal reboot. All34 original shell files
remain as components and compatibility sources, not the complete active flow.
Explicit cancellation/deletion controls and
queued-work ownership checks are covered by separate candidate evidence.
Revision 4 corrects the DiPO CP owner for same-type phone switching and the
BtSettings deferred deletion. persist.leafia.owner.r4bridge=0 turns off only
the new detach of another phone's live Bluetooth iAP2 attach; the other
revision-4 owner corrections (watchdog, supersede/cancel, bridge address,

also added lane capture and experimental lane encodings to GPM, Navi_ExSL and
DiPO (its lane modes 1 to 4 are retired by revision 5, below) and optional
CarPlay lane identification to Iap2ServerApp (persist.leafia.cp.lanes, default

(persist.leafia.lane.log unset = on, 0 = off): the three apps, including the
stock navigation app, append to /data/data/<app>/files/leafia-lane.log (at most
2 x 512 KB per app). Restore does not delete these lane log files. In revision
4 these five apps changed and the other seven functional outputs remained
identical to revision 3. Every verified restore resets the five
persist.leafia.* switches to unset. The revision-4 updater also restored an
installed .03-r3 from its own lifecycle archive before installing.
Revision 5 (lanes-v2) changes only the lane helper in GPM, Navi_ExSL and DiPO:

No full manufacturer APK, ODEX, JAR, or native library is shipped. The package
rebuilds apps from the head unit's own exact stock data and applies the original
hash-gated native recipes. The Recovery supplies code and catalogs only and
requires a verified device-side lifecycle archive with the original baseline.
Normal native removal may drop registration even after a hook failure; the
external archive survives and no failed-restore reboot is armed. The new manual
Recovery validates every required original before writes and restores/validates
again after manager cleanup. It cannot restore NEEV.KOR.01 or older custom
transaction backups.

Build/source integrity and host fixture tests do not establish vehicle success.
The original software's installed message and a host fixture reboot request do
not prove actual projection, navigation, audio, Wi-Fi, or post-reboot operation.
